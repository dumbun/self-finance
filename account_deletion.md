## 1. What Data Can Be Deleted?

Self-Finance stores data in two locations: locally on your device, and (if you signed in using Google Sign-In) in our hosted Firebase cloud backend (Google Cloud).

### A. Local Data (On Your Physical Device)
* You can delete individual customer profiles, transaction entries, item records, and photos directly inside the App by using the edit/delete options on each screen.
* To completely remove all local data, clear the application storage/cache in your Android/iOS settings or uninstall the App.

### B. Cloud Account & Cloud-Synchronized Data
When you request account deletion, we permanently delete all data linked to your account in our cloud backend (Google Firebase), including:
1. **User Account & Authentication Profile:** Your Google email address, display name, profile avatar URL, and internal authentication UUID.
2. **Customer Records:** All customer names, guardian names, phone numbers, addresses, and notes entered under your account.
3. **Ledger & Transaction History:** All loan records, repayment histories, interest calculations, balance entries, and timestamps.
4. **Collateral & Pawn Data:** All pawned item descriptions, weights, purity records, and valuations.
5. **Uploaded Media & Attachments:** All customer photographs, customer identification proof documents (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN card, driver’s license), collateral item photos, and on-screen customer digital signatures stored in our cloud storage buckets.

## 2. Step-by-Step Instructions to Request Account & Data Deletion

To request the complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data:

### Step 1: Compose an Email
* Send an email to: **[sf.help.vamshi@gmail.com](mailto:sf.help.vamshi@gmail.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request%20-%20Self-Finance)**
* **IMPORTANT:** You **must** send the email from the exact Google Account email address you used to sign in to the Self-Finance app. This is required to verify your identity and ensure unauthorized parties cannot request deletion of your financial records.

### Step 2: Use the Following Email Template
* **Subject Line:** `Account Deletion Request - Self-Finance`
* **Email Body:**
  ```text
  Hello Self-Finance Support,

  I am requesting the permanent deletion of my Self-Finance account and all associated cloud data stored on your servers.

  My Account Details:
  - Registered Google Email: [Enter your Google email address here]
  - Approximate date account created (optional): [e.g., 2026]

  I understand that this action is irreversible and that all my synced customer records, transactions, photos, and signatures will be permanently erased.

  Thank you.
  ```

### Step 3: Verification & Execution
* Upon receiving your email, we will verify that the request originated from the registered account email address.
* We will process and permanently delete your account, database records, and cloud storage files within **30 days**.
* You will receive a confirmation email once your account and all associated cloud data have been completely purged from our servers.

## 3. Data Retention & Exceptions

* **Irreversible Action:** Cloud account deletion is permanent. Once wiped, your data cannot be recovered.
* **Pre-Deletion Backup:** If you wish to keep a copy of your transaction history before deleting your account, open the Self-Finance App and use the **Export to CSV** feature located in the **Settings** menu.
* **Local Copies:** Deleting your cloud account does not automatically delete data stored locally on your device. To delete local copies, uninstall the App or clear app storage in your device settings.
* **No Marketing Retention:** We do not retain personal information for marketing, as we do not conduct marketing or sell data. We retain only minimal email correspondence records necessary to prove compliance with your deletion request.
