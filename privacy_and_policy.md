# Self-Finance Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 24 October 2026**

Self-Finance (“Self-Finance,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a mobile application for managing customer, loan, item, payment, and transaction records. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

**Developer:** Kamsali Vamshi Krishna<br>
**Contact:** [sf.help.vamshi@gmail.com](mailto:sf.help.vamshi@gmail.com)

## 1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, information entered into Self-Finance can include:

- Customer names, guardian names, addresses, and phone numbers.
- Loan or pawned-item descriptions, dates, amounts, interest, payment details, and transaction history.
- Customer or item photos, proof images, and transaction signatures that you choose to add.
- Your app profile information returned by Google when you sign in, such as your name, email address, and account identifier.

You are responsible for having the authority and any required permission to enter information about other people, and for providing any notices required by law.

## 2. Local storage and cloud sync

App records are stored in a local database and files on your device so you can use the app on that device. Self-Finance also offers cloud account and synchronization features. When you sign in and the app syncs, it sends supported records and attachments to our Supabase-hosted services, associated with your account. Sync can occur when you request it and when the app detects an internet connection. Synced records can include the information listed in Section 1, record identifiers, and timestamps needed to sync updates and deletions.

Cloud sync means your data is transmitted to and stored by Supabase; it is not an offline-only app. Review your account and sync settings before entering sensitive information. If you do not want records sent to the cloud, do not sign in or use sync. Some account and sync features will then be unavailable.

## 3. Device features and exports

- **Camera and photo library:** If you choose to add a photo or proof image, the app uses the camera or photo selection feature you invoke. The selected image is stored with your app records and may be uploaded with those records when sync is used.
- **Biometric unlock:** If enabled, fingerprint or face verification is performed by your device’s operating system. The app does not receive or store your biometric template; it receives the device’s authentication result.
- **Notifications:** Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device based on app records and your notification preferences. The app does not use these local reminders as push advertising.
- **CSV/PDF and sharing:** When you export or share records, the app creates a file for the destination or sharing method you select. The receiving app or service then handles that file under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **Phone, SMS, and email links:** If you choose to call, text, or email from the app, your device opens the relevant app with the details you selected.

Self-Finance does not request access to your address book as part of the customer list feature; customer records are entered into the app.

## 4. How information is used

Information is used to provide app features, authenticate your account, save and sync records, display reports and calculations, create reminders and exports you request, maintain service security, and diagnose or resolve operational problems. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The app does not intentionally use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

## 5. Service providers and sharing

We use **Supabase** for authentication, database hosting, file storage, and synchronization. When you choose Google sign-in, **Google** participates in authentication and handles information under Google’s privacy terms. These providers process information to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.

Exports and content you choose to share are sent to the destination you select. We do not otherwise sell, rent, or share app records for another party’s advertising.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Cloud records remain associated with your account until deleted through an available in-app action or following a verified deletion request. To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, email [sf.help.vamshi@gmail.com](mailto:sf.help.vamshi@gmail.com) from your account email and identify the account. We may retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate security and dispute-resolution purposes. Deleting a cloud copy does not remove exports already saved elsewhere or data held by a recipient app.

Before uninstalling or changing devices, export or sync records you want to keep. Uninstalling may remove local-only information.

## 7. Security and international processing

We use Supabase authentication and transport encryption provided by the service for cloud communications, together with access controls intended to protect stored data. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Service providers may process or store information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their infrastructure and applicable law.

## 8. Children

Self-Finance is intended for business and personal recordkeeping by adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to sign in, sync, grant device permissions, enable notifications or biometric unlock, and create exports. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete account information by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights over your personal information under applicable law.
